Vesuvius Challenge — PHerc0191 First Letters campaign

Research log: every run, what it asked, what it showed, what changed

Goal

Find 10 legible letters in a 4 cm² area of an unopened Herculaneum scroll (First Letters, $50k/scroll), and surface evidence-backed tooling fixes along the way (Progress Prizes).

Scroll

PHerc0191 — 9.362 µm / 113 keV scan 20250821151635, crushed cross-section, full public data (CT, Lasagna normals, tracks, surface predictions). Not in the community's minimal-route sweeps.

Hardware

4-GPU Linux box (2× RTX 3060 12 GB, RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB; GTX 1660 SUPER unusable), i3-9100 4 cores, 1 TB NVMe, 300 Mbps WAN.

Latest

#26Pass
Positive control: the PHerc. 343 winning segment through our pipeline

Our pipeline can see prize-grade ink, but only with the ensemble (or Hecate) and a row-scale view. A single model at whole-winding scale would have missed it, so the single-model nulls on w069–w072 are not evidence of absence. The PHerc. 0191 search must use the ensemble.

#25Partial
Geometry triage of all snapped windings (flatten + 66-layer render + CT surface layer)

If this holds across the band, several hundred cm² of searchable sheet exist from one 1,000-slice fit. The ink search, not the geometry, is now the bottleneck.

#24Partial
Snapped + flattened w070: 66-layer render and first ink-ensemble depths

The recipe produces geometry good enough for an ink search. One depth of one winding is not a search: the letters in 343 appeared at d = +1 on one winding of many. Next: geometry triage of all 100 snapped windings, then sweeps on the ones that look like this. Update after the PHerc. 343 positive control: a single model at whole-winding scale misses real ink, so this is not yet evidence of absence; re-check with the full ensemble.

Findings

About the scroll and the geometry

  • The ink detector is not the problem

    Calibrated on known text: three legible Greek lines. All PHerc0191 nulls are geometry.

  • Positive control passes only with the ensemble

    On the PHerc. 343 winning segment our four-model ensemble shows the letter rows; ink_9um seed 42 alone at whole-segment scale does not. Searches on 0191 need the ensemble (Hecate is the strongest single model).

  • Minimal-route spiral fits sit on the sheet ~46–49% of the time

    Satisfaction = within 6 voxels and 0.45 winding of the track's sheet. Weights don't help; 3× steps gives +3 points.

  • The fitted spiral never reaches the outer third

    Per-sector audit: outermost winding 3–8 mm short of the boundary in the fat sectors; initial spacing fixes the radial extent; stretching hurts the inner tracks.

  • Crushed scroll = same winding count, 2.5× spacing variation by sector

    0.13 mm on the flattened sides (ray audit under-counts there) to 0.26 mm at the ends. Needs anisotropic deformation or local spacing supervision.

  • Patch growing needs its normal grids

    Without normal_grid_path the tracer produces data-independent discs. With them it partly follows sheets.

  • The PHerc. 343 First Letters win (2026-10-08) used our pipeline plus three steps we lacked

    Spiral fit with the surface-prediction distance field, no shell, 30k steps; post-hoc snap of every vertex onto the nearest predicted sheet (±15 vox); 66-layer render swept over 31 depths through a four-model ink ensemble. All open source; adopted (see notes/prior-art-digest.md).

About the tools (Progress Prize material)

  • #1 libvolcomp not installed by the VC3D build (fixed, PR #2008)

    One-line CMake install fix; submitted.

  • #2–#5 image tags, lockfile rewrite, remote-fetch aborts

    :stable tag missing; uv.lock rewritten by sync; infer and renderer abort on a single transient remote chunk fetch.

  • #6 Shipped track-crossing cache rejected after download

    Signature uses nanosecond mtime; any download zeroes sub-second mtime. 2.4 GB downloaded for nothing; every run rebuilds.

  • #7 Outer-shell loss silently zero for sparse shells

    Confidence normalized by an edge spike → 0.22 < 0.25 → no valid samples, no warning. Reproduced outside the fitter.

  • #8 Fiber inference needs lasagna on PYTHONPATH

    geometry.py imports lasagna.omezarr_pyramid unguarded; the models extra does not ship it.

  • Upstream removed the inputs the winner used (Spiral simplification, 2026-09-23)

    surf-SDT support and the 'phase' dense-spacing mode were deleted from spiral-fitting three weeks before they won a prize. We run the pre-removal commit in a worktree to reproduce the route.

  • #9 Resident-pool sidecars reused after the source store is rebuilt

    The fitter silently trained on a stale SDT pool (1 of 1,456 bricks) after the store was rebuilt at the same path.

  • #10 Raw spiral windings render 23–33% compressed

    Meshes declare a 20-voxel grid but nodes are 26–30 voxels apart; ink models see letters too small unless the winding is flattened first. Mesh areas are understated ~2×.

Pilot spiral fit, z 9000–10000, 10k steps

2026-10-08Baseline
On-sheet track points0.461
Tracks fully satisfied0.083

Question

How well does the minimal-route spiral fit (umbilicus + Lasagna + tracks) follow PHerc0191's sheets?

Technique

spiral-fitting fit_spiral.py, dense_spacing_mode grad_mag, tracks on, no patches/shell/winding model. 46 min on one RTX 3060.

Result

120 windings exported; on-sheet track points 46%, fully satisfied tracks 8%. Renders of w040/w070/w100 are on-sheet only in patches; ink maps are noise.

What we learned

A point counts as on-sheet only within 6 voxels and 0.45 winding of its track's sheet, so more than half the track points are off the fitted surface. Ink is noise until that changes.

Outer shell from the CT mask (sparse) + outer winding 105

2026-10-09Bug found
On-sheet track points0.458-0.003
Tracks fully satisfied0.081

Question

Does telling the fitter where the papyrus ends, and how many windings there really are (~100 by ray audit), fix the outer region?

Technique

make_outer_shell.py: boundary of the masked CT every 8 slices → tifxyz; shell_outer_winding_idx 105.

Result

No change — the shell loss was silently zero all run.

What we learned

Tooling finding #7: the fitter normalizes shell confidence by its global max; a shell sampled every 8 slices lands every interior value at 0.22, below the 0.25 threshold, so no sample is valid and the loss is 0 with no warning.

Fiber-direction inference on the band (SD2 model)

2026-10-09Done

Question

Produce the direction field the tracer needs.

Technique

vesuvius fiber_trace_3d.infer, level 0, z 8500–10500, output scaled down 4×; 1.6 h on one 3060 after fixing a missing-module import (tooling finding #8).

Result

nx/ny/presence pyramids. The output is a sheet NORMAL field in Lasagna's compact encoding, not raw fibre directions; the tracer needs an x/y/z layout, so a converter was written.

Two encodings, one formula ((u8−128)/127), different layouts. Converted with symlinks plus a computed z component.
